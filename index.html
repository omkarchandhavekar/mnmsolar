Frequently Asked Questions

Important Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed information about solar costs, subsidies, net-metering and monsoon performance.

With a properly sized on-grid rooftop solar system, your electricity bill reduces by 80% to 90%. Excess electricity generated during the day is exported to the MSEDCL grid and credited against your nighttime consumption.
Under PM Surya Ghar Yojana, subsidies are ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above, directly credited to your bank account. M & M Solar handles all approvals.
Yes! Solar panels work on light (photons), not heat. During monsoon, you get 25% to 40% of peak output. Excess credits from sunny months cover monsoon consumption.
Approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free roof space per kW. So a 3 kW system needs about 240 to 300 sq. ft.
Solar PV systems have no moving mechanical parts, which makes them remarkably low maintenance. All they require is periodic washing with clean water every 2 to 3 weeks to remove accumulated dust or leaves. M & M Solar also offers complete Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for electrical health checkups and inverter diagnostics.
