Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about rooftop solar costs, subsidies, net-metering, and panel durability in Maharashtra.

A properly sized on-grid rooftop solar system typically eliminates 80% to 90% of your electricity bill. Under Maharashtra's net-metering policy, units produced by your panels that are not consumed during the day are exported back to the grid and adjusted against your nighttime power usage.

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers receive ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and up to ₹78,000 for a 3 kW or larger system. M & M Solar handles the complete portal registration, inspection, and subsidy disbursement assistance directly into your bank account.

Yes! Solar photovoltaic panels work on daylight (photons), not direct heat. While production during heavy rain is around 25% - 40% of peak sunny days, high-efficiency Mono PERC panels used by M & M Solar continue generating electricity. Excess units generated during sunny months (Oct to May) remain credited in your MSEDCL net meter to balance monsoon consumption!

As a standard rule of thumb, each 1 kW of solar capacity requires approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop space. Therefore, a 3 kW system requires roughly 240 to 300 square feet of flat or sloped roof area.

Solar PV systems have no moving mechanical parts, which makes them remarkably low maintenance. All they require is periodic washing with clean water every 2 to 3 weeks to remove accumulated dust or leaves. M & M Solar also offers complete Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for electrical health checkups and inverter diagnostics.
